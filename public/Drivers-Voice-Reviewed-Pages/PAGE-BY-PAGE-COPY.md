# Page-by-page revised copy

These introductions are included in the revised source files. Shared homepage, independence, priority and survey wording is in `_forum-copy.ts`. Actual records and dates must come from authorised sources, not this document.

## /about

Our aim is to help drivers communicate constructively about pay, rosters, working time and workplace support. Drivers Voice does not decide whether an employer, union or individual has broken the law.

A forum coordinator can help organise information and requests. That role does not automatically give anyone formal negotiating or union authority.

## /admin/actions

Record an agreed action, the outcome requested, responsible role and next review date. Do not turn an allegation or an unanswered question into a statement of fault. Public summaries need separate review.

## /admin/audit

Use audit entries to review access and changes. An entry records an event reported by the system; it does not by itself prove the lawfulness of access or the truth of a report. Do not copy report narratives into audit notes.

## /admin/members

Invite eligible drivers using the minimum information needed. Grant only the roles required, review access when responsibilities change and record the reason for privileged access. Forum approval is not proof of union membership.

## /admin

Use this overview to coordinate authorised work, identify reviews due and keep follow-up organised. Access should follow a person’s assigned responsibilities, not committee membership alone.

## /admin/process-tracker

Track confirmed process information and requests for updates. Record the source and date for each confirmed entry. If the process or referral has not been confirmed, leave it unconfirmed.

Do not publish references, correspondence, settlement proposals, admissions or mediation material. Ask the authorised representative what may be shared for the actual process. A private portal is not an exemption from confidentiality obligations.

## /admin/reports/[id]

Keep the driver’s account, supporting sources and reviewer assessment distinct. Mark only the specific factual point actually checked. Record what remains uncertain and avoid conclusions about guilt, liability or credibility that the forum is not equipped to determine.

Where an issue belongs in a formal grievance, safeguarding, safety, protected-disclosure or legal process, help identify the appropriate route without republishing the underlying material.

## /admin/reports

Review only records within your authorised remit. Triage for urgency and the right reporting route before assessing a submission. Do not treat a report as a finding or disclose it more widely merely because it concerns a common issue.

## /admin/settings

The person or group deciding why and how records are processed must establish the privacy arrangements. Completing this screen or obtaining two internal confirmations does not certify GDPR compliance.

Document an actual lawful basis and any required sensitive-data condition. Explain retention, recipients, rights and contact routes accurately. Verify both the notice and live access controls before enabling collection.

## /admin/surveys

Use rankings to describe consultation participation, not to claim that all drivers support a demand. State the survey dates, number of valid responses and method where results are published.

Suppress small or identifiable combinations and review free text separately. Never describe account-linked responses as anonymous or present this consultation as a statutory or union ballot.

## /admin/updates

Write each update for a defined audience and check factual accuracy, necessity, privacy and any process restrictions before publication. A source check is an editorial step, not legal clearance.

Exclude identifying allegations and confidential dispute material from public drafts. Do not publish information merely because a submitter agreed to share it; other people’s rights and existing restrictions may still apply.

## /admin/volunteers

Assess expressions of interest against the agreed selection process. Keep contact information restricted and record any decision separately from an application. Applying does not appoint a representative or authorise publication of a profile.

## /code-of-conduct

Drivers may disagree, question decisions and raise concerns respectfully. These rules protect fair participation; they must not be used to suppress a good-faith concern or lawful contact with a representative, adviser, regulator or public authority.

## /contact

Use this form for a question about the forum or a correction to website content. Signed-in drivers should use the workplace-concern form for an individual employment issue. Passenger service queries belong on the company’s official website.

This inbox is not an emergency channel. Avoid sending incident files, medical information or confidential negotiation material.

## /our-plan

The aim is a reliable system: hear from drivers across shifts, identify common concerns, agree what to ask for, and report back on progress. A suggestion becomes an agreed position only through the appropriate participation and approval process.

## /

A place for drivers to turn everyday concerns into clear questions, agreed priorities and practical follow-up. Taking part is voluntary. Different views are welcome.

## /portal/account

Check the account details used by the forum and sign out after using a shared device. A display name does not make an authenticated submission anonymous. Do not share sign-in links or verification codes.

## /portal/actions

Follow the actions agreed for this forum: the requested outcome, responsible role, target date and latest update. A target date is a planning commitment, not a legal deadline.

An overdue review means an update is needed. It does not establish that the company, a union official or a driver has refused to act.

## /portal/incidents/new

Use this form to request follow-up after the relevant official reporting steps have been taken. Record only the minimum needed to explain what support or response is outstanding.

For immediate danger or an urgent safety concern, use the applicable emergency, control, engineering and official safety channels now. This forum is not monitored for emergencies.

Do not include passenger or child identities, witness contact lists, CCTV, detailed injury information or live operational-security details. Ask for a private callback through an appropriate representative where necessary.

## /portal

Welcome to your driver hub. Here you can raise a concern, request incident follow-up, take part in consultation and read approved member updates. Other drivers do not need access to your individual report for the group to discuss a common theme.

A forum report does not notify the company, file a WRC complaint or extend any legal deadline. Seek timely advice if a deadline or urgent issue may apply.

## /portal/priorities

Choose the three topics that matter most to you. You may edit a saved ranking while the survey remains open. Participation is voluntary and nobody should pressure you to rank a particular issue.

This account-based consultation is confidential within the published access rules, not anonymous. It is not a formal union ballot or authority for industrial action.

## /portal/questions

Ask how the forum works, request clarification or identify something that needs correcting. Give the page title or update reference where possible. Your message is a request for review, not a public comment.

## /portal/reports/[id]

This page contains your submitted account and the forum’s follow-up record. A source check does not establish that an allegation is true or determine anyone’s legal rights. You can ask for a correction or review without changing the historical record silently.

## /portal/reports/new

Tell us what happened, when it happened, how it affected your work and what outcome you would like. Separate what you personally observed from information you received from others.

Include only necessary details. Do not upload or copy confidential company records, passenger information or another person’s medical details. Use the appropriate company or representative channel for sensitive material.

This form is not a formal grievance, accident report, protected-disclosure channel or WRC complaint. It does not preserve or extend a claim deadline.

## /portal/reports

View the concerns and follow-up records linked to your account. A status shows the forum’s handling stage, not a company decision, finding of fault or guarantee of resolution.

## /portal/updates/[slug]

Read this update in the context of its date, intended audience and any stated uncertainty. Member access does not make confidential dispute material suitable for circulation. Request clarification rather than filling gaps with rumours.

## /portal/updates

These updates are approved for their stated audience. Member-only material should not be forwarded or screenshotted into public groups. If wider sharing is appropriate, request a separately approved public summary.

## /portal/volunteer

Help with listening to drivers, organising records, checking sources or preparing updates. Expressing interest does not appoint you as a committee member, shop steward or negotiator.

Any profile proposed for publication must be reviewed separately. Keep personal contact details out of a public candidate statement.

## /priorities

Tell us what would make the biggest practical difference to your working life. Survey responses help inform discussion; they do not, by themselves, create a negotiating mandate or settle a dispute.

## /privacy

Please read the current privacy notice before using a form. Signing in identifies your account; this service is not anonymous. Access restrictions reduce exposure but do not provide absolute confidentiality or legal privilege.

## /sign-in

Use the email address linked to your invitation. Do not use a company payroll, staff-portal or union password. Access approval confirms eligibility for this forum only; it does not confirm union membership or a representative appointment.

## /updates/[slug]

This is a Drivers Voice update. A source-checked label records an editorial review; it is not a legal finding or a statement issued by the company, SIPTU or the WRC. Use the contact page to request a correction.

## /updates

Read updates approved by the forum for public release. Each update should explain its source, what is known, what is still being checked and when it will be reviewed. Approval by the forum is not approval by the company, SIPTU or the WRC.
